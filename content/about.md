Welcome to my home page! I am a Master student in Communication Science at the
[School of Journalism & Communication](https://www.tsjc.tsinghua.edu.cn/),
[Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), where I work as a research
assistant with Dr. Shuang Chen. I received my B.A. in Communication from
[Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/) in 2024, graduating in the top
1% of my class with the Distinguished Honor Graduate Award, and spent a year as an
exchange student at
[Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://www.lmu.de/en/).

My research sits at the intersection of **media psychology** and **interpersonal
communication**. I study how everyday digital media reshape family relationships and
young people's well-being — including parental phubbing and parent–adolescent
closeness, adolescents' mobile disconnection and resilience, and bystander behavior
in cyberbullying. More recently, I have become interested in how generative AI enters
intimate settings such as parenting, health, and fact-checking. My undergraduate
thesis examined how different types of online community participation relate to
maternal guilt. Methodologically, I work with longitudinal surveys, experiments,
experience sampling, and in-depth interviews, using SPSS, Mplus, and R.

Outside of research, I play the **guzheng** (Level 10 certificate) and was a member of
the Zhejiang University Folk Orchestra. I swim with the **Tsinghua University Swimming
Team**, hold a national third-level swimmer certification and a national fifth-level
lifeguard license, and I have recently taken up surfing.
