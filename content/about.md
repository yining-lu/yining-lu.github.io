Welcome to my home page! I am a Master student in Communication Science at the
[School of Journalism & Communication](https://www.tsjc.tsinghua.edu.cn/),
[Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), where I am advised by
[Dr. Shuang Chen](https://www.tsjc.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/info/1029/1354.htm). I received
my B.A. in Communication from
[Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/) in 2024, graduating in the top
1% of my major with the Distinguished Honor Graduate Award, and spent half year as an
exchange student at
[Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://www.lmu.de/en/).

My research sits at the intersection of **media psychology** and **interpersonal
communication**. I examine how digital technologies become embedded in everyday
relationships and, in turn, shape well-being and social behavior. My work focuses
particularly on families and young people, including parental technology use and
parent–child communication, adolescents' digital disconnection and mental health, and
responses to online risks such as cyberbullying. More recently, I have become
interested in how generative AI enters family. Methodologically, I use surveys,
experiments, experience sampling, and in-depth interviews, and analyze both
longitudinal and dyadic data.

Outside of research, I play the **guzheng** (Level 10) and was a member of the Wenqin
Chinese Orchestra of Zhejiang University
([One of our performances](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y9iTEtw0iJk)). I also swim
with the **Tsinghua University Swimming Team** and hold national Level III swimmer and
Level V lifeguard certifications. During my internship with the
[United Nations Office for Project Services (UNOPS)](https://www.unops.org/) in
Indonesia, I quickly fell in love with surfing.
